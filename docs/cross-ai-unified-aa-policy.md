# Unified AA: distinguish admission target from executable minimum

Precommitted scope, 2026-09-08. This is a bounded capital-allocation experiment,
not an accepted candidate. It continues Z without changing any qualification,
frozen economic threshold, strategic grant, repair authorization, fee or order
definition.

## Evidence and single change

Y Ownership remove-sz300394 has four fully observed NORMAL/unfrozen sessions
with qualified candidates, available slots and adequate concentration room.
Actual cash is 11.83%, 11.90%, 12.25% and 18.62% of equity. Calculated unreserved
cash equals actual cash; there is no duplicate reservation. All four
ordinary admissions fail the 20% minimum despite exceeding the existing 5%
minimum trade weight. This is a policy restriction, not an accounting error.

The unified allocator already computes the executable increment after cash,
pending BUY reservations, gross, symbol, industry and correlation limits.
Ordinary admission currently uses `core_admission_weight` both as the desired
budget and as an all-or-nothing minimum. AA retains it as the desired budget
and uses existing `min_trade_weight` as the minimum acceptable increment.
Execution continues to enforce native lot sizes, costs and minimum notional.

This does not force a sale or free capital from an unfilled SELL. Existing
healthy holdings remain held. Strategic grants and bounded frozen-account
repair budgets retain their existing minimum/permission requirements. No
ordinary holding receives strategic identity. Existing partially filled BUY
qualification checks and settled-epoch constraints remain unchanged.

## Precommitted validation and limits

First reproduce the normal-risk residual-capital rejection with native orders
and fills. Verify that sub-minimum cash, missing qualification and frozen risk
do not buy; verify existing shares and pending reservations remain intact.
Then run affected allocation modules and the champion plus the previously
failed remove-sz300394 Ownership scenario, using complete current-source raw
evidence. This is not the full matrix and is not permission to relax the
Ownership first-authorization or actual-crown definitions.

Z's complete observations contain 26 qualifying residual-budget checks in the
champion starting July 2025, so a fresh champion replay is necessary. Z H2 and
the existing Y H1 observations contain no such checks in their full 125/118
sessions. AA has no identified mechanism to repair those two known failures;
do not rerun them merely because a session changed or label their old results
as AA acceptance. The remaining recovery/entry capability work is separate.

Smaller entries can increase orders and costs, consume cash before a better
candidate arrives, or worsen drawdown. Improved utilization alone is not a
successful economic outcome. No result is claimed at precommit time.
