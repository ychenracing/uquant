existing CORE budgets but funds a nondominant FULL_COHORT from the entire group
budget even when its individually qualified members remain immature. In the
complete H2 observations, two immature reversal members each receive one third;
the third witness fails its own entry conditions and receives no capital.

**dominant** evidence and receives 95%. Maturity alone cannot justify treating
these formations identically, nor does a losing H2 prove reduced capital wins.

For a new FULL_COHORT with no existing decisive dominant, cap each immature
member's desired weight at min(original desired, core_admission_weight). Use
that member's actual current maturity. Mature members retain their original
desired weight. Do not redistribute withheld capital or increase smaller
original requests. All current individual eligibility and shared funding limits
remain. Genuine dominant, restricted pair/single and final cash-rearm authority
budgets retain their existing behavior.

This is the granted target for this deployment, not a temporary cap with a
promised automatic maturity top-up. FULL_COHORT already enters ACTIVE on actual
owner BUY and has no ordinary CORE promotion path. Do not invoke the existing
single-owner promotion on a group or reconstruct a larger past authorization.
Normal price-drift holding and valid risk-compression restoration remain within
the actual granted authority. Existing ACTIVE accounts are not retrospectively
resized; partial BUYs and restarts retain the same original real target/identity.
This intentionally forgoes additional initial exposure, including in successful
trends. No new state, threshold, security/date exception or parallel allocator.
